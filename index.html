How a review works

Built around the challenge process referees already follow, so a review gets faster without changing who decides.

  1. The referee taps the challenge type

    In or out, a touch at the block, and the other calls coaches can challenge.

  2. Osprey cues the right angle

    The best camera angle opens at the moment that matters, instead of someone scrubbing through footage.

  3. Osprey Vision adds the evidence

    Ball tracking and physics add data points to the review, such as where the ball landed or whether its path changed at a block.

Then the referee decides.

Osprey supplies evidence. It never makes or announces a call.

What Osprey Vision shows

A great replay tech can have the play cued up at the right angle and the right moment. What they can't show is the physics behind the play. Osprey Vision tracks the ball and measures it, and it's built to help with every challenge review, not just line calls and block touches.

A great replay tech

  • Finds the best camera
  • Cues up the right moment
  • Shows the referee the frame

Osprey Vision

  • Best angle and moment, cued in one tap
  • Tracks the ball's speed and path
  • Shows the physics behind the play: how the ball landed, the mark it left, whether its path changed

Two examples from this fall

Where it landed

Osprey Vision tracks the ball to the floor and shows the mark it left against the line, so a line call rests on the contact itself.

Whether the block touched it

Vision compares the ball's path before and after the blocker's hands. A change in direction is evidence of a touch, even when no angle shows the fingertips.

What goes into Osprey

One system with three parts. Each conference or school chooses its own replay equipment and cameras. Osprey gives a conference one standard across every member gym, and gives its officials better material to learn from.

  • Osprey Perch

    Optimal camera placement

    Every arena is different. Send measurements and photos of your gym, and Perch returns a camera placement plan, all done remotely, so every gym in your conference gives referees the same angles.

  • Osprey Hover

    Post-game reviews

    For conferences to review challenges and for referee training, built from the challenges in your own matches.

  • Osprey Strike

    Full live Osprey Vision

    Real-time feeds, instant replay review and live decision support.
